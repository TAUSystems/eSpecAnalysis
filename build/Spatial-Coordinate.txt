Lab coordinates for divergence and pointing angle calculation.
Electron Source is at origin (x,y,z)->(0,0,0)

Pointing:
z: Laser Propagation Axis
y: Vertical Axis (g points in negative Y-Dir)
x: Horizontal Axis (right hand rule)
phi: angle from x-axis in XZ-plane. phi = 0 -> alignment with x-axis.
theta: angle from y-axis in YZ-plane. theta = 0 -> alignment with y-axis.
Note: Do not confuse with in code xAxis, yAxis. These are image coordinates for horizontal, and vertical respectively.
(x,y,z,phi,theta) are used to transform from image coordinates into lab coordinates.
Position of screen is for image coordinate zero position.
